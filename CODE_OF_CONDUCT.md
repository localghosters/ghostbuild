# Code of Conduct

GhostBuild is a community project maintained under LocalGhosters. We want it to be
a welcoming place to ask questions, report problems and contribute.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. In practice:

- Assume good faith and be patient with people who are new to the project or to reproducible builds.
- Give specific, kind feedback on code and ideas, not on people.
- Accept that people disagree, and discuss disagreements on technical merits.
- Do not harass, insult, demean or discriminate against anyone.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other channel run for the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who
break these rules. To report a problem, open a private security/conduct report
through the repository's "Report" feature on GitHub, or contact the maintainers
at the address listed on the repository once one is published.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
